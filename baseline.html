Baseline Screener

A short check that covers every Reading & Writing skill, so we know exactly what to work on first.

22 questions · 22 minutes.

Two questions on each of the eleven skills, all at the test's middle difficulty. Work at a steady pace — you won't see whether an answer was right until the end, which is normal for a check like this.

Afterwards there's an optional five-minute follow-up that pins down how far your strongest skills go and whether the weak ones need re-teaching or just practice.
